TORQX Automotive

Premium automotive accessories for drivers who demand more.

TORQX Automotive is a modern automotive accessories brand focused on
practical upgrades, vehicle styling, everyday convenience, technology,
protection, and road-ready essentials.

The TORQX storefront is designed as a premium digital showroom where
customers can explore automotive products through clean product
photography, organized collections, detailed product pages, and a fast,
responsive shopping experience.

🚗 Brand Overview

Brand: TORQX
Company: TORQX Automotive
Industry: Automotive Accessories & Vehicle Lifestyle
Location: Detroit, Michigan, United States
Website: https://torqx-automotive.com

Brand Positioning

TORQX combines automotive utility with modern design. The store focuses
on products that help drivers personalize, protect, organize, upgrade,
and enhance their vehicles without turning the shopping experience into
a traditional auto-parts catalog.

Brand Direction

TORQX uses a dark, high-contrast automotive visual identity with
metallic elements and red accents. The overall direction is inspired by
performance vehicles, modern garages, automotive technology, and premium
aftermarket culture.

🏁 Store Categories

TORQX organizes its catalog into clear product collections:

Interior

Car Tech

Phone mounts, chargers, wireless accessories, digital accessories, dash

Car Care

Cleaning products, detailing accessories, microfiber products, interior

Audio

Speakers, audio accessories, adapters, mounts, cables, and products

Road Trip

charging equipment, and long-drive essentials.

Performance Accessories

Vehicle accessories focused on driving experience, appearance,
convenience, and aftermarket customization.

Gift & Essentials

Useful automotive gifts and everyday accessories for drivers, commuters,

📸 Photography Guidelines

TORQX product imagery should follow a consistent standard:

Realistic product photography

Clean backgrounds

Product centered and completely visible

Correct proportions

No unnecessary cropping

No screenshots of ecommerce pages

No prices embedded into photographs

No buttons or UI elements inside images

No promotional text baked into product images

No unrelated products in the same product frame

Consistent presentation across the catalog

Each product should have its own intentionally assigned image.
